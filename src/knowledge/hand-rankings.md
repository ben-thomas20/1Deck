# Hand Rankings in Texas Hold'em

Poker hands are ranked from highest to lowest as follows. When two players have the same type of hand, tiebreakers determine the winner.

## 1. Royal Flush
The best possible hand: A, K, Q, J, 10 all of the same suit.
Example: A♠ K♠ Q♠ J♠ 10♠
There is no tiebreaker — all royal flushes are equal (split pot).

## 2. Straight Flush
Five consecutive cards of the same suit.
Example: 9♥ 8♥ 7♥ 6♥ 5♥ (9-high straight flush)
Tiebreaker: The highest card in the straight wins. A-2-3-4-5 suited (steel wheel) is the lowest straight flush.

## 3. Four of a Kind (Quads)
Four cards of the same rank plus one other card (kicker).
Example: K♠ K♥ K♦ K♣ 7♠
Tiebreaker: Higher four of a kind wins. If equal (community quads), the highest kicker wins.

## 4. Full House (Boat)
Three cards of one rank plus two cards of another rank.
Example: Q♠ Q♥ Q♦ 8♣ 8♠ (Queens full of eights)
Tiebreaker: Higher three of a kind wins first. If equal, higher pair wins.

## 5. Flush
Five cards of the same suit, not in sequence.
Example: A♦ J♦ 8♦ 6♦ 3♦
Tiebreaker: Compare cards from highest to lowest. All five cards matter for tiebreaking.

## 6. Straight
Five consecutive cards of mixed suits.
Example: 9♠ 8♥ 7♦ 6♣ 5♠
Tiebreaker: Highest card in the straight wins. The ace can be high (A-K-Q-J-10) or low (A-2-3-4-5, called "the wheel"). The wheel is the lowest straight.

## 7. Three of a Kind (Trips / Set)
Three cards of the same rank plus two unrelated cards.
Example: J♠ J♥ J♦ 9♣ 4♠
"Set" means you have a pocket pair and one matches the board. "Trips" means two are on the board. Sets are generally stronger because they're harder to detect.
Tiebreaker: Higher three of a kind wins. If equal, compare kickers.

## 8. Two Pair
Two different pairs plus one other card.
Example: K♠ K♥ 9♦ 9♣ 5♠
Tiebreaker: Higher pair wins. If top pairs are equal, compare second pairs. If both pairs are equal, compare kickers.

## 9. One Pair
Two cards of the same rank plus three unrelated cards.
Example: 10♠ 10♥ A♦ 7♣ 3♠
Tiebreaker: Higher pair wins. If pairs are equal, compare kickers from highest to lowest (up to three kickers matter).

## 10. High Card
No pair, no straight, no flush — just the highest card.
Example: A♠ J♥ 8♦ 5♣ 2♠
Tiebreaker: Compare cards from highest to lowest. All five cards matter.

## Important Notes
- In Texas Hold'em, you make the best 5-card hand from 7 available cards (2 hole cards + 5 community cards).
- Suits have no ranking in Hold'em. A flush of hearts is equal to a flush of spades of the same ranks.
- If all five community cards make the best hand and no player can improve on it, the pot is split equally.
