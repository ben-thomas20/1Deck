# Poker Terminology Glossary

## Game Structure
- **Blinds**: Forced bets posted by two players to the left of the dealer button. The small blind is half the big blind.
- **Button (Dealer Button)**: A marker that indicates the nominal dealer. Rotates clockwise each hand. The best position at the table.
- **Ante**: A small forced bet from all players, sometimes used in later stages of tournaments.
- **Buy-in**: The amount of chips a player starts with at a cash game table.
- **Pot**: The total amount of chips bet during a hand.

## Hand Phases
- **Preflop**: The first betting round after players receive their two hole cards.
- **Flop**: Three community cards dealt face up, followed by a betting round.
- **Turn (Fourth Street)**: A single community card dealt after the flop, followed by a betting round.
- **River (Fifth Street)**: The final community card, followed by the last betting round.
- **Showdown**: When remaining players reveal their hands to determine the winner.

## Player Actions
- **Fold**: Discard your hand and forfeit the pot. You lose any chips already bet.
- **Check**: Pass the action without betting. Only legal when no bet is outstanding.
- **Call**: Match the current bet to stay in the hand.
- **Raise**: Increase the current bet. Other players must call the new amount, re-raise, or fold.
- **All-in**: Bet all of your remaining chips.
- **Limp**: Calling the big blind preflop without raising. Generally considered a weak play.

## Hand Types
- **Hole Cards (Pocket Cards)**: The two private cards dealt to each player.
- **Community Cards (Board)**: The five shared cards in the center of the table.
- **Pocket Pair**: When your two hole cards are the same rank (e.g., 9♠ 9♥).
- **Suited**: Two cards of the same suit (e.g., A♣ K♣). Notated with "s" (AKs).
- **Offsuit**: Two cards of different suits (e.g., A♣ K♥). Notated with "o" (AKo).
- **Connectors**: Two cards of consecutive rank (e.g., 8-7, J-T).
- **Broadway**: Any card ten or higher (T, J, Q, K, A).

## Pot and Odds
- **Pot Odds**: The ratio of the current pot to the cost of calling a bet.
- **Implied Odds**: Pot odds adjusted for the money you expect to win on future streets.
- **Equity**: Your percentage chance of winning the pot based on your current cards and possible outcomes.
- **Outs**: Cards remaining in the deck that will improve your hand.
- **Drawing Dead**: Having no outs — no remaining card can make you the winner.

## Betting Terminology
- **Continuation Bet (C-Bet)**: A bet on the flop by the preflop raiser.
- **Value Bet**: A bet made with a strong hand, hoping to be called by a weaker hand.
- **Bluff**: A bet made with a weak hand to try to make opponents fold.
- **Semi-Bluff**: A bet with a drawing hand that could improve to the best hand.
- **Check-Raise**: Checking and then raising after an opponent bets.
- **Donk Bet**: Betting into the preflop raiser out of position. Named because it's generally considered a novice play.
- **Overbet**: Betting more than the size of the pot.
- **Minimum Raise (Min-Raise)**: Raising by the smallest legal amount.

## Strategy Terms
- **Tight**: Playing few hands; selective.
- **Loose**: Playing many hands; not selective.
- **Aggressive**: Betting and raising frequently.
- **Passive**: Checking and calling frequently.
- **TAG (Tight-Aggressive)**: The most profitable style for beginners — play few hands but play them aggressively.
- **LAG (Loose-Aggressive)**: Playing many hands aggressively. Requires more skill to be profitable.
- **Nit**: An extremely tight player who only plays premium hands.
- **Calling Station**: A player who calls too frequently and rarely raises or folds.
- **Range**: The set of all possible hands a player could hold in a given situation.
- **Tilt**: Playing emotionally and making poor decisions, usually after a bad beat.

## Pot Types
- **Main Pot**: The primary pot that all active players are eligible for.
- **Side Pot**: A separate pot created when a player goes all-in for less than the total bet. Only players who contributed to the side pot can win it.
- **Split Pot (Chop)**: When two or more players have hands of equal value, the pot is divided equally.

## Table Positions
- **Under the Gun (UTG)**: First to act preflop, left of the big blind.
- **Cutoff (CO)**: One seat to the right of the button.
- **Hijack (HJ)**: Two seats to the right of the button.
- **Early Position**: UTG and adjacent seats.
- **Late Position**: Button and cutoff.
